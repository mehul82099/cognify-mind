## How This Board Works

- **Every morning**, `startup-daily-kickoff` reads this board, finds today's tasks, assigns them to the right agent, and logs the kickoff.
- **Every task** has an owner. Owner executes, commits to repo, updates the board.
- **Blocked tasks** are escalated to Sulbha. She decides: wait, reassign, or drop.
- **Done tasks** get committed with a clear commit message.
