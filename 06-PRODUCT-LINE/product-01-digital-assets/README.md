## What the Bot Does

1. **Greets customers** in your brand voice when they message the bot.
2. **Answers FAQs** from your list — instantly, accurately, in your tone.
3. **Handles general questions** using the AI model — with the brand voice you set.
4. **Escalates when unsure** — detects confusion, uncertainty, or complex requests, and routes to you with context.
5. **Logs interactions** — so you can see what customers are asking and improve over time.

---

## What It Doesn't Do

- It doesn't book appointments (that's a separate skill pack — coming soon).
- It doesn't process payments (not yet).
- It doesn't replace a human entirely — it augments you. Escalation is built in.
