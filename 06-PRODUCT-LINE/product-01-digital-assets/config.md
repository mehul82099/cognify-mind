## How to Customize This Pack

### 1. Business Name
Replace `(the customer fills this in)` with the business name.
Example: `My Salon` or `Acme Web Design`

### 2. Brand Voice
Pick one of these, or write your own:

**Option A — Friendly and direct:**
> "Hi! I'm the assistant for [Business Name]. I can help with questions about our services, pricing, and setup. If I don't know the answer, I'll connect you with a human."

**Option B — Warm and professional:**
> "Welcome to [Business Name]. I'm here to help. Here's what I can answer, and if something needs a human, I'll make sure you get to one."

**Option C — Short and quick:**
> "Hi, I'm the [Business Name] bot. Ask me anything about our services — I'll answer or connect you with a person if needed."

### 3. FAQ List
Edit `FAQ_LIST.md` to add your business's common questions and answers.

### 4. Escalation Contact
Set how the bot reaches you when it escalates:
- **Telegram:** Your Telegram username (e.g., `@yourname`)
- **Email:** Your email address
- **Phone:** Your phone number (if you want SMS escalation — advanced)

### 5. Channel
By default, the bot works on Telegram (the channel Hermès is connected to). If you want it on WhatsApp, Discord, or another channel, connect that channel in Hermès and the same skills work.

## What the Customer Does After Buying

1. Install Hermès Agent (free).
2. Create a Telegram bot via @BotFather (free, 2 minutes).
3. Connect the bot to Hermès (follow the setup guide).
4. Edit `config.md` with their business name, brand voice, FAQ list, and escalation contact.
5. Edit `FAQ_LIST.md` with their actual FAQs.
6. Start the bot. It's live.

Total setup time: ~30 minutes.
