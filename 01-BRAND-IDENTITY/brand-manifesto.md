## Brand Manifesto

We are Mystify Me. We build AI agents that do real work — research, respond, create, automate — for free, using entirely open-source and free tools. No subscriptions. No gatekeeping. No "paywall your own agent."

We believe:
- **AI should work for you 24/7**, not just when you're at your keyboard.
- **Voice is the future** — agents that talk, not just type.
- **Content is the marketing** — show, don't tell. YouTube + Instagram, organic, relentless.
- **Free tools are enough** — if we can't build it with free open-source tools, we haven't tried hard enough.
- **Transparency wins** — show the work, show the stack, show the results.

We serve: creators, solopreneurs, small business owners, anyone who wants AI that does things — not just chats.

## Brand Voice & Tone

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Confident, clear, slightly mysterious (we "mystify"), but never arrogant |
| **Voice** | Female-led (Sulbha = CEO voice), direct, warm but professional |
| **Style** | Short sentences. Active voice. Show results, not promises. |
| **Vocabulary** | Plain English. No jargon without explanation. "Here's exactly what we built." |
| **Visual identity** | Dark/mysterious palette with pops of vibrant color. Clean, modern, slightly enigmatic. |

### Voice Rules
- **Do:** Lead with what was built. Show the work. Be specific.
- **Do:** Speak like a capable human, not a corporate bot.
- **Don't:** Overpromise. Don't use hype words without substance.
- **Don't:** Sound like a SaaS sales page. We're builders, not sellers.

---

## Brand Visual Language

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Colors** | Dark base (#0a0a0f or similar), accent: electric purple/cyan gradient, white text |
| **Typography** | Clean sans-serif (Inter, Space Grotesk, or similar free fonts) |
| **Logo concept** | Something that suggests "mystification" + "AI" — abstract, memorable, works in small sizes |
| **Thumbnails** | High contrast, 2-3 words max, face optional but human connection helps |
| **Instagram aesthetic** | Consistent dark theme, clean layouts, work-in-progress shots, results-first |

### Free Tools for Branding
- Logo/icon: Figma (free), GIMP, or AI generation (Stable Diffusion)
- Thumbnails: Canva free tier, GIMP
- Color palette: Pick once, document in this repo, use everywhere
- Fonts: Google Fonts (free) — Inter, Space Grotesk, Fraunces
